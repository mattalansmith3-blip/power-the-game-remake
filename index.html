    POWERThe Game · remake

    Plan secret orders. Everyone moves at once. Capture a Flag to win.

    How to play
    1. Win: walk an Infantry into an enemy HQ and keep it there to the end of the round (infantry you capture in that same fight don't count). You take their Flag, they're out, and all their units become yours. Last player standing wins.
    2. Each round you give up to 5 secret orders, then press GO!. The AIs plan in secret too, and then everyone moves at the same time.
    3. Orders: Move units from a space to a neighbouring space · Merge 3 of a kind in one space into a Mega unit · Buy a new unit.
    4. Units: Infantry 2 (land, the only flag-taker) · Tank 3 (land) · Destroyer 4 (sea) · Fighter 5 (land and sea). Mega: Infantry 10 · Tank 15 · Destroyer 20 · Fighter 25.
    5. Fights: when forces share a space, the bigger total power captures every loser — they change colour and join the winner. Nothing is destroyed. A tie: the attackers turn back. Armies that swap places fight on the road.
    6. Power points: +1 every round, and +2 for each neutral or foreign land space you held for the whole round (your own country earns nothing).
    7. Buying: Infantry 2, Tank 3, Destroyer 4, Fighter 5. New units appear at your HQ (Destroyers at your home port) and can't move until next round.
    8. Tips: keep a guard at your HQ, march Infantry with escorts, merge into big stacks, and watch the neighbour who's gathering power.
